## Lesson 1 — Hotels (excerpt, full transcript in local transcripts/01-hotels.txt)

Search by city/area (not a specific hotel) for a broader, better-priced
result set. Each listing shows the average public-platform price next to the
member price and the savings percentage. Travel Credits earned on the
booking are shown per-listing; the lesson states credits post **5-7 days
after the stay is completed**. A map view and a draggable "little yellow
man" give a 3D street-level view of the hotel. Listings can be added to a
wish list for later. The hotel detail page shows distances to nearby points
of interest (malls, landmarks, the nearest airport) and the room-type detail
page shows cancellation deadlines/penalties per rate. Elite members can
apply Loyalty Points for an extra discount on top of the listed savings, and
can also redeem Travel Credits at checkout. Local/destination taxes are
collected at check-in, not at booking, and apply to everyone regardless of
membership tier.

## Lesson 2 — Flights (excerpt)

Choosing the "All Airports" option for a city (e.g. "New York All Airports")
searches every airport near that city and compares prices, rather than
locking the search to one specific airport. The platform's own search caps
are an explicit filter option: maximum layover 24 hours, maximum total trip
duration 72 hours. Baggage allowance (checked bag weight, already included
in the shown price) is listed per flight segment. As an Elite member, a
booking needs at least one named passenger who is either the account owner
or one of up to 4 registered additional travelers; every other passenger on
the same booking can be an unlimited number of one-time guests added
directly on the reservation (name, DOB, country, document number). Airline
check-in itself is done on the airline's own website/app using the booking
code from the confirmation email — not on Travel Advantage. For some
(especially low-cost) airlines, Travel Advantage sends a separate follow-up
email with the specific check-in process; airline check-in windows commonly
open only 24-48 hours before departure.

## Lesson 3 — Car Rentals (excerpt)

Pick-up/drop-off location, driver's country of citizenship (affects
insurance terms and price) and driver age (commonly a threshold around 25)
all affect price and eligibility. The quoted price at booking is a
reservation only — **payment is collected when the car is picked up**, not
at the time of booking. Extras (GPS/navigation, child/baby seats, additional
insurance) are selected and paid for locally at pick-up, not during the
online reservation. Some vehicles are offered with unlimited mileage; this
is called out per listing, not guaranteed for every car. Booking online in
advance is explicitly recommended as cheaper than arranging a rental
in-person without a prior reservation.

## Lesson 4 — Transfers (excerpt)

Point-to-point transfers are not limited to airport-to-hotel trips — usable
for any point A to point B movement, including within the same city on a
daily basis. Vehicle class (economy/estate/van/business) affects price.
Loyalty Points and/or Travel Credits can be applied at checkout and can
reduce the price of a transfer to as low as $0 in a favorable case shown in
the lesson.

## Lesson 5 — Cruises (excerpt)

Search flow narrows step by step: region → departure port → cruise line →
sailing length/date, each step narrowing subsequent options. Listings can
include a 360°/video preview of the ship, deck plans, and 360° stateroom
views. Cabin type (inside/outside/balcony/suite) is chosen after selecting
an itinerary, with a live countdown timer to complete the booking at the
quoted price. Paid add-ons available during checkout include internet
packages (priced per device/per night), specialty-restaurant packages, and
photo packages; gratuities/service charges may already be included in the
quoted price (stated per-cruise) with cruise taxes shown separately.

## Lesson 6 — Life Experiences (excerpt)

Two tiers exist: standard Life Experience and **Deluxe** Life Experience
(Deluxe includes more activities during the stay). A Life Experience
typically includes a **Life Experience Host** present at the destination to
help members check in and navigate the stay, plus a welcome
mixer/reception for members attending the same Life Experience to meet each
other. **Shared occupancy**: a member who is not travelling with a second
Member/traveler of their own can choose "shared occupancy" when booking;
the first member to book a room receives a shared booking code, which a
second, independent member enters on their own booking to be roomed
together with the first. After booking, a Life Experience reservation shows
as "pending" status until shortly before the travel date, when it is
confirmed — this is described as normal, not an error.

## Lesson 7 — Lifestyle Mall (excerpt)

An online shop module. Country selection at entry determines which
warehouse(s)/products are available to ship to that country — not every
product ships everywhere. A single order can combine products from
different warehouses, arriving as separate packages, at no extra shipping
charge, as long as the order total is **at or above $150** (free shipping
threshold stated in the lesson). Purchases earn **Travel Credits** and
Elite members can apply **Loyalty Points** for an additional discount on
Lifestyle Mall purchases, the same mechanic used on travel bookings.
Flash Deals inside the Mall are described as deep (50-70%+) discounts on
select name-brand items.

## Lesson 8 — Resorts (excerpt)

Search flow: region → country/area → dates with a day-flexibility window
(e.g. ±7 days) the platform uses to widen the deal search. Some resorts are
flagged **"all inclusive mandatory"**: for these, the price shown at search
time does **not** include the mandatory all-inclusive fee — that fee is
listed separately on the listing and is collected on arrival at the resort,
not at booking.

## Lesson 9 — Activities (excerpt)

Activities are available in most destinations worldwide, including
day-to-day activities in a member's own city, not only vacation
destinations. Filterable by category (e.g. food & drink, tours). Loyalty
Points can be applied at checkout and, depending on balance, can cover the
full cost of some activities. Confirmed activity bookings are sent as a
voucher by email, which is presented at the activity location.

## Lesson 10 — Stays (excerpt)

"Stays" lists houses/apartments (whole-unit, often with a kitchen/living
room) as an alternative to hotel/resort rooms, aimed at families or longer
stays. Cancellation terms (refundable up to a stated date, after which a
penalty applies) are shown per listing, same pattern as Hotels. Loyalty
Points and Travel Credits can be applied at checkout; Travel Credits are
earned once the stay is completed.
